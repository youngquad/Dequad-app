## 1. Executive Summary

**DEQUAD** is a UK-headquartered software platform that helps university students build genuine peer connections, monitor their daily wellbeing, and access proactive safeguarding — all within a closed, identity-verified network of UK higher-education institutions.

We are addressing three converging national crises:

- **Loneliness epidemic** — 54% of UK undergraduates report frequent loneliness (ONS, 2023).
- **Mental-health crisis** — 1 in 3 students experience a clinically significant mental-health issue during their degree (Student Minds, 2024).
- **Safeguarding gap** — Average wait to see a university counsellor is 6 weeks; only 23% of UK universities offer 24/7 crisis support.

DEQUAD is the only platform in the UK that combines **verified-student peer matching**, **daily mood analytics**, and **machine-assisted safeguarding** into a single closed-network product, free at the point of use for students and monetised through institutional subscriptions, B2C premium tiers, and aggregated wellbeing analytics for university leadership teams.

## 2. Description of Products and Services

### 2.1 The DEQUAD platform

DEQUAD is delivered as a cross-platform application (iOS, Android, web at dequad.co.uk) and a backend API. The product has four production modules:

| Module | What it does | Innovation |
|---|---|---|
| **Verified peer matching** | Match students with compatible peers for friendship, study groups, and peer support — only within `.ac.uk` student-email-verified accounts. | Closed-network identity + intent-based matching (friendship / study / peer-support) — not present in Bumble BFF, Hinge, or Facebook Groups. |
| **Daily wellbeing tracker** | Lightweight 30-second daily mood check-in capturing mood, sleep, stress and connection. Generates a personal trend dashboard. | Anonymised, opt-in, ICO-DPIA-cleared; pairs the trend with peer-connection data to flag emerging isolation. |
| **Machine-assisted safeguarding** | LLM-powered classifier on top of mood and chat metadata flags suicide-risk, harassment, hate-speech, and disordered eating signals to designated university safeguarding contacts. | First UK student platform to combine `.ac.uk` identity verification with proactive safeguarding triage. |
| **University Insights Dashboard** | Aggregated, anonymised wellbeing analytics for university staff: cohort mood trends, engagement, hotspot alerts. | Lets institutions monitor wellbeing at population scale without breaching individual privacy. |

### 2.2 What the customer buys

| Customer | What they receive | How they pay |
|---|---|---|
| **UK university** | Site-licence to the University Insights Dashboard, branded safeguarding alerts pipe, dedicated success manager. | Annual SaaS subscription from **£12,000 / year**, tiered by student headcount. |
| **NHS Integrated Care Boards (ICBs)** | Population-level mood/engagement data for the institutions they fund, plus joint safeguarding referral path. | Annual contract from **£20,000 / year** per ICB. |
| **Student (consumer)** | Free core product (matching, mood, basic chat). Optional **DEQUAD Premium** for advanced filters, unlimited chats, profile boost. | **£4.99 / month** subscription, billed via Stripe. |

### 2.3 Why students choose DEQUAD

- **Trusted by your university** — only verified `.ac.uk` students, staff and alumni-pretenders are blocked.
- **Built around wellbeing**, not romance — clear product positioning differentiates from Bumble/Hinge.
- **Privacy-first** — mood data is private to the student; only aggregated, anonymised data reaches universities.
- **Crisis-ready** — safeguarding referrals are routed to a real human at the student's institution in under 60 seconds.

## 3. Innovation, Viability and Scalability

Endorsement requires evidence that the business meets all three Home Office criteria. Each is addressed below.

### 3.1 Innovation

DEQUAD is a **genuinely original business plan** with **unmet need** in the UK university market:

1. **Closed-network identity verification** — DEQUAD is the only UK app that enforces `.ac.uk` student-domain verification plus an explicit student-status attestation, then routes ambiguous accounts to a human admin queue. Existing competitors (Bumble BFF, Hinge, Facebook Groups, Reddit) do not verify university affiliation.
2. **Integrated safeguarding pipeline** — DEQUAD's machine-assisted classifier feeds directly into existing university safeguarding teams via a webhook and is the only consumer-facing student app with this institutional integration.
3. **Wellbeing-first product positioning** — competitors are either dating-led (Bumble), study-led (UniBuddy), or generic social (Discord). DEQUAD is the only product positioning loneliness and wellbeing as the headline use case for university-age students.

### 3.2 Viability

DEQUAD's viability rests on:

- **A production-ready MVP** already deployed at dequad.co.uk with ~50 beta users from 3 institutions.
- **Two founders with complementary, university-tested skill sets** (see Section 7 and Appendix CVs).
- **A revenue model proven in adjacent markets** — Togetherall and Talkcampus both charge UK universities £15k–£60k/year for related (but inferior) products and have 100+ UK university customers between them.
- **Modest capital required** — £3,000 founder capital + a credible £150k pre-seed bridge in Q3 Y1 once first paid pilots are signed.
- **Realistic growth assumptions** — 3 paying universities by end of Y1 (consistent with Togetherall's own first-year metrics).

### 3.3 Scalability

DEQUAD scales because:

- **Software-only marginal cost** — incremental student users cost £0.07/month in hosting and zero in support, so gross margin reaches 86% by Y3.
- **Network effects within an institution** — each new student raises the value of the platform to every other student at the same university, accelerating intra-institutional adoption.
- **Cross-institutional roll-out path** — onboarding a new university requires a 6-week implementation cycle; we project 24 institutions live by end of Y3 vs. 285 UK universities total.
- **International expansion optionality** — the closed-network model maps cleanly to `.edu`, `.edu.au` and EU university domains, opening a £180m TAM in EN-language higher-education markets after Y3.
