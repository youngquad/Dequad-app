UKES Outreach Emails — Pre-Application Sequence

A sequence of three emails for engaging UK Endorsement Services Ltd (UKES) ahead of formally submitting the Innovator Founder endorsement application. The sequence is designed to:

  1. Open a respectful, brief first contact;
  2. Request a 30-minute pre-application call (which UKES typically welcomes);
  3. Confirm the call and pre-share the executive summary.

The applicant should send these from a professional UK domain (e.g. quadri.yusuf@dequad.com) and reference the live product at https://dequad.co.uk.

Email 1 — Initial enquiry

To: info@ukendorsement.com (or contact form at https://ukendorsement.com) CC: none Subject: Pre-application enquiry — Innovator Founder endorsement for DEQUAD (live product, UK-focused)

Dear UK Endorsement Services team,

My name is Yusuf Quadri. I am the founder of DEQUAD (https://dequad.co.uk), a UK-headquartered safeguarding-first student-wellbeing platform that I have built to a live, working state in production and now intend to scale through UK university partnerships.

I am writing to enquire about UKES’s Innovator Founder endorsement assessment process. Before formally submitting an application I would like to confirm:

  1. That DEQUAD is the kind of business UKES typically considers under the Innovator Founder route;
  2. The current published assessment fee and typical timeline from submission to decision;
  3. Whether a brief (30-minute) pre-application call would be possible to walk through DEQUAD’s position against the Home Office’s three statutory criteria.

A short summary of the business:

  • Live, working product at https://dequad.co.uk (FastAPI backend, React Native / Expo native + web frontends, MongoDB persistence). I have built the platform end-to-end as sole technical founder.
  • Verified-student peer network (.ac.uk email gate), per-user wellbeing baseline detection, safeguarding pipeline with escalation to a Designated Safeguarding Lead, and 24/7 support chat.
  • Two-sided business model: free for students, paid by universities (£1.50–£2.80 per student per year) and NHS Integrated Care Boards from Year 3.
  • 5-year UK plan: 100 partner universities, 42 UK FTE jobs created, break-even Q3 Year 3.

A full endorsement submission document plus 13 supporting appendices is ready to share.

I am happy to send the executive summary and a 1-page deck in advance of any call.

Thank you for considering this enquiry. I look forward to hearing from you.

Tone-and-style notes for the applicant

  • Be brief. UKES assessors review hundreds of pitches. Aim for emails that fit on one screen.
  • Lead with the live product link. A working product is the strongest credibility signal an Innovator Founder applicant can bring.
  • Use UK English (organisation, not organization; pilot programme, not program).
  • Avoid hype words. UKES is allergic to “revolutionary”, “disruptive”, “Uber-for-X”. Stick to verifiable claims.
  • Don’t request a decision in writing without paying the assessment fee. UKES’s published process requires a paid submission to obtain a written assessment.
