## 1 · Executive Summary

**The problem.** 54% of UK university students report frequent loneliness (ONS 2023). 1 in 3 develop a mental-health condition during their degree (Student Minds 2024). University counselling waits average 6 weeks. The Office for Students' Condition B3 and the HE Mental Health Charter now require **whole-university** peer-support coverage that no existing UK product provides.

**The product.** DEQUAD is a closed mobile + web platform for verified UK students. Three integrated services:

1. **Verified peer matching** based on course, society, and lifestyle — not photos.
2. **Daily 10-second mood check-in** building a per-user wellbeing baseline.
3. **Safeguarding-led messaging** with real-time crisis-keyword scanning + escalation to a trained DSL.

**The business model.** Free for students. Universities pay £1.50–£2.80 per student per year — **below the cost of expanding in-house counselling**. NHS ICB commissioning from Year 3.

**The ask.** Endorsement under the Innovator Founder route to formally launch DEQUAD Ltd from a UK base, hire 42 UK FTE over 5 years, and deliver measurable mental-health outcomes for UK university students.

## 3 · What DEQUAD Does

### Closed-network peer matching
- Sign-up gated by active `.ac.uk` email
- Compatibility based on course, modules, society interests, study habits, lifestyle
- **Not a dating app** — focus on friendship, study groups, society discovery
- Every message scanned in real time

### Per-user wellbeing baseline
- Daily 10-second mood check-in
- First 14 entries calibrate the user's personal baseline (mean, sd)
- Deterioration from baseline (z < −1.5 for 2 days) triggers a **private** in-app nudge
- Methodology paper outline in Appendix D

### Safeguarding & 24/7 support
- Crisis-keyword scan on every message + mood note
- High-risk content → instant alert to Designated Safeguarding Lead (DEQUAD's own + partner university's)
- 24/7 chat-based first-line support (machine-assisted first reply; human handover for serious cases)
- Live "Report a profile" feature with 4-hour human-triage SLA

### Institutional dashboard
- Anonymised, aggregate-only metrics for partner-university wellbeing teams
- **No individual student data ever surfaced** to universities — hard contractual commitment

## 4 · Innovation (Criterion 1)

DEQUAD's innovation is the **combination of three things no UK competitor has integrated**:

| Layer | What it is | Why it's novel |
|---|---|---|
| **Closed-network safeguarding** | `.ac.uk`-only network with proactive scanning that escalates to *the user's own university's* DSL | Converts safeguarding from a moderation cost-centre into a regulatory compliance *value*-centre for the partner university |
| **Per-user wellbeing baseline** | Statistical detection of deviation from a personal mood baseline (not population threshold) | Reduces false positives for chronically-low users + false negatives for normally-cheerful users — both safety-critical (see Appendix D) |
| **Two-sided revenue architecture** | Free for students; paid by universities and NHS ICBs | Removes the consumer paywall that kills most mental-health apps while creating sustainable revenue |

**Competitive moat formed:**
- First-mover in UK closed-network student safeguarding (no incumbent)
- Network-effect defensibility — value to any one student rises with each verified peer at their university
- Data moat — per-user baselines accumulate value over time; switching costs grow with usage
- Brand positioning in the *safeguarding* category, not the *dating* category — competitors cannot duplicate without architectural change

## 6 · Scalability (Criterion 3)

### Three-horizon plan

**Horizon 1 — UK Universities (Y1–Y2):** 25 partner universities by end of Y2. ~250,000 verified students (≈9% of UK HE population). Direct sales to Heads of Student Services; partnerships with UUK, AMOSSHE, HE Mental Health Charter network.

**Horizon 2 — UK NHS & Public Sector (Y2–Y4):** 5 NHS ICBs commission DEQUAD as part of their Long Term Plan 16–25 mental-health offer. Adjacent verticals: FE colleges, sixth-form, apprenticeships. NHS Innovation Accelerator (Y2 application); NICE Early Value Assessment.

**Horizon 3 — International (Y4–Y5):** Republic of Ireland first (English-speaking, identical HE structure), then Australia/NZ, then Canada. Verification localisation only — language stays English in Phase 1.

### Why DEQUAD scales

| Scaling lever | Detail |
|---|---|
| Infrastructure-light | Adding a new university = one config change. Cloud-native, auto-scaling Kubernetes |
| Marginal cost per active user | ~£0.05 per active user per year |
| Safeguarding scales linearly | Each new university brings its own DSL → DEQUAD's central staffing stays constant |
| Two-sided revenue | Doesn't require consumer ad spend; sells via institutional procurement frameworks (JISC, NHS) |
