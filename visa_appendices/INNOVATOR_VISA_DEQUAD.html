1. Executive Summary

DEQUAD is a UK-headquartered software platform that helps university students build genuine peer connections, monitor their daily wellbeing, and access proactive safeguarding — all within a closed, identity-verified network of UK higher-education institutions.

DEQUAD addresses three converging national crises:

  • The student loneliness epidemic: 54% of UK university students report frequent loneliness (ONS, 2023).
  • The student mental-health crisis: One in three undergraduates experience a clinically significant mental-health issue during their degree (Student Minds, 2024).
  • The university safeguarding gap: Existing university counselling services are over-subscribed (average wait time: 6 weeks) and lack 24/7 reach.

DEQUAD is the only platform in the UK that combines verified-student peer networking, daily mood analytics, and machine-assisted safeguarding into a single product that is free at the point of use for students and monetised via institutional subscriptions to universities, NHS Integrated Care Boards (ICBs), and selected premium features.

The business is live in production at https://dequad.co.uk, with a working full-stack platform (backend, web, iOS, and Android in beta), 12 seeded test universities, and a working safeguarding pipeline. The product is ready for institutional pilots in academic year 2026/27.

The applicant is requesting endorsement on the basis that DEQUAD meets the Home Office’s three statutory criteria for the Innovator Founder route: innovation, viability, and scalability. Detailed evidence is presented in Sections 5, 6, and 7 respectively.

3. The DEQUAD Business

3.1 What DEQUAD does

DEQUAD is a multi-platform software product (mobile + web) that delivers three integrated services to UK university students:

  1. Verified peer matching — Students sign up with their .ac.uk university email. Once verified, they are matched with peers based on course, modules, society interests, study habits, and lifestyle compatibility. The matching prioritises platonic friendship, study partnerships, and society-mate discovery; it is not a dating app.

  2. Daily wellbeing tracking — Users log a 10-second mood check-in each day. The system establishes a personal wellbeing baseline; deviation from baseline triggers proactive support nudges (signposting Samaritans, Shout, NHS Every Mind Matters, and the user’s university counselling service).

  3. Safeguarding-led messaging & support — All in-app messages are scanned in real time for crisis language, harassment, and exploitation indicators. Confirmed high-risk content is escalated to a trained safeguarding lead (initially DEQUAD’s own designated lead; eventually the partner university’s). A 24/7 chat-based support function provides instant first-line response, with human handover for serious cases.

3.2 What DEQUAD is NOT

DEQUAD is not a dating app, a clinical service, a replacement for university counselling, or a free-for-all social network. The product is deliberately narrower, slower, and stricter than incumbents — that constraint is the basis of its trust premium with both students and institutions.

4.2 The institutional pain point

UK universities and the Office for Students (OfS) face mounting regulatory pressure to demonstrate proactive student welfare. The Higher Education Mental Health Charter (2022) — now adopted by 90+ UK institutions — requires whole-university approaches that include peer support and out-of-hours coverage. Universities currently meet this requirement with a patchwork of:

  • In-house counselling (capacity-constrained)
  • Third-party crisis lines (high cost-per-call, reactive)
  • Generic student-life apps (no safeguarding integration)

DEQUAD fills the proactive, peer-mediated, out-of-hours segment of this stack — a category no existing UK competitor occupies at scale.

4.3 Why now

  1. Post-pandemic loneliness has remained elevated. Cohort surveys 2022–2024 show student social-connection scores have not returned to pre-2020 baseline.
  2. The Online Safety Act 2023 (Section 12 + Schedule 11) has made existing US-based social platforms increasingly costly and risky for under-25 user bases. UK-domiciled, safeguarding-first products have a regulatory tailwind.
  3. University budgets are shifting from in-house counselling toward digital wellbeing tooling: the JISC HE Digital Wellbeing Report (2024) found 78% of UK universities plan to procure third-party wellbeing platforms in 2025–2027.

5. Innovation Assessment (Home Office Criterion 1)

“The applicant has a genuine, original business plan that meets new or existing market needs and/or creates a competitive advantage.”

DEQUAD’s innovation is in three combined layers that no existing UK product unifies:

5.1 The closed-network safeguarding model

Most social apps are open networks moderated reactively (after a report) by anonymous external moderators. DEQUAD is a closed network restricted to verified .ac.uk students, with proactive scanning that escalates to trained, named safeguarding leads at the user’s own institution. This converts safeguarding from a cost-centre (moderation) to a value-centre (regulatory compliance for the partner university). No incumbent — Tinder, Bumble BFF, Yubo, Meetup, or university-app vendors like Unitu — offers this combination.

5.2 The personal-baseline wellbeing engine

Generic mood-tracking apps (Daylio, Moodnotes) rely on absolute scoring. DEQUAD’s engine builds a per-user baseline from the first 14 daily check-ins and detects statistically meaningful deviation. This dramatically reduces false positives compared to threshold-based systems and is, to our knowledge, novel in the student wellbeing space. (See Appendix D for the methodology paper outline.)

5.3 The institutional-revenue / student-free architecture

The product is free for students (removing the access barrier that kills most mental-health apps) but generates revenue from university and ICB licences. This two-sided model is novel in the UK student wellbeing space, where competitors are either:

  • Free + ad-funded (mis-aligned incentives, e.g. TalkLife)
  • Direct-to-consumer paid (Calm, Headspace — low penetration among students)
  • B2B with no student-facing app (Spectrum.Life, Big Health — institutional dashboards only, no peer matching)

7. Scalability Assessment (Home Office Criterion 3)

“The applicant has a clear pathway to scaling the business, creating jobs in the UK, and reaching national or international markets.”

7.1 Scalability of the underlying product

DEQUAD is infrastructure-light by design:

  • Cloud-native architecture (Kubernetes via Emergent, with documented migration path to AWS/Azure) supports horizontal scaling without code changes.
  • Per-user marginal cost is dominated by storage (~£0.04 per active user per year) and outbound email/push (~£0.01 per active user per year) — see Appendix F for unit-economics.
  • Adding a new university requires a single configuration change (email-domain allow-list) and no code deployment.
  • The safeguarding engine scales linearly: each new university brings its own Designated Safeguarding Lead, so DEQUAD’s central staffing burden remains constant.

14. Safeguarding, Regulatory & Compliance Framework

14.1 Safeguarding policy

DEQUAD operates under a written Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) framework modelled on the UUK Stepchange guidance and the Suicide-Safer Universities toolkit:

  1. All staff (founders + employees + contractors) complete Level 2 Safeguarding Adults training before user data access.
  2. A named DSL (qualified, DBS-checked) is on call during operating hours; an out-of-hours protocol routes to Samaritans / Shout / 999 as appropriate.
  3. Confirmed high-risk events are logged with timestamp, action taken, and outcome — auditable by partner-university DSLs.
  4. Annual independent safeguarding audit performed by a third-party (e.g. Survivors UK Consulting, NSPCC Learning).

14.2 Relevant UK regulatory frameworks

  • UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018
  • Online Safety Act 2023 — Sections 10, 12, Schedule 11
  • Equality Act 2010 — accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA committed for all client interfaces)
  • Mental Health Act 1983 (as amended) — DEQUAD does not provide regulated clinical care; clear in-product disclaimers
  • Care Act 2014 — partner-university DSL alignment for adult safeguarding referrals
  • NHS DSPT — toolkit target Year 3

19. Social Impact & UK Public Benefit

DEQUAD delivers measurable social value to the United Kingdom beyond commercial revenue:

  • Reduction in student crisis presentations: Pilot universities will report A&E mental-health presentations among matched DEQUAD users vs control cohort; early modelling targets 12–18% reduction.
  • Earlier identification of at-risk students: Per-user baseline detection surfaces deterioration ~3 weeks earlier than self-report alone.
  • Cost saving for NHS: Independent modelling (LSE Health & PSSRU framework) estimates £6 of NHS service avoidance per £1 spent on proactive peer-wellbeing programmes.
  • UK skills development: 42 high-quality UK tech / clinical / commercial jobs by Year 5; commitment to UK university apprenticeships from Year 2.
  • Diversity: DEQUAD is founded by a person of colour and is committed to a hiring policy that mirrors the UK student demographic mix.
