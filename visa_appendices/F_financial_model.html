Key Assumptions & Risks

ASSUMPTIONS & RISKS
1.Institutional pricing £2/enrolled student/yr (£20k for 10,000-student university). Below in-house counsellor cost (£40+/student); validated against JISC 2024 procurement data.
2.Premium pricing £4.99/mo (£59.88/yr). Y1 = 60 beta converts; Y2+ ~3% of registered base. Below Calm (5.7%) and Headspace (4.2%) consumer benchmarks.
3.University procurement cycle 4–6 months from first contact (JISC HE buying data). Bedfordshire pilot mid-year conversion = 0.5 avg paid-year in Y1.
4.Pre-seed and seed are TARGETS, not committed funds. Business survives M1–M6 on £6k founder capital with positive month-end balances throughout. Each funding event is gated on a delivered milestone.
5.Both founders fund personal living costs from existing employment (CEO: Change Grow Live; CTO: freelance engineering). No founder salary drawn M1–M6.
6.Y3 net cash outflow of £37.5k reflects planned investment in scaling to 10 institutions. Closing balance £731k provides 16+ months runway at Y3 burn rate and bridges to Series A.
7.Break-even target Q2 Y4 after Series A deployed and institutional ARR reaches required scale.
8.NHS ICB first pilot Y3 (£10k fee); full commissioning scale Y4+ contingent on NHS Innovation Accelerator acceptance (~30% acceptance rate).
9.R&D tax credit (HMRC RDEC) ~16% on qualifying spend; modelled conservatively at 10% effective rate.
