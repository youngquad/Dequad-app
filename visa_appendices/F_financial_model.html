KEY ASSUMPTIONS & RISKS
1. Pricing assumes £1.50-£2.80 per student/year — below in-house counsellor cost (£40+ per student); validated against JISC 2024 procurement data
2. Conversion to premium = 3-5% of verified students; below Calm (5.7%) and Headspace (4.2%) consumer benchmarks
3. University procurement cycle = 4-6 months from first contact; aligns with JISC HE buying data
4. NHS ICB commissioning gated on NHS Innovation Accelerator acceptance (~30% acceptance rate)
5. Cost figures use 2026 London salary benchmarks; +3% annual inflation applied
6. International revenue (Y4+) assumes Republic of Ireland first (no localisation cost); ANZ requires localisation
7. R&D tax credit (HMRC RDEC) of ~16% available on qualifying spend; modelled conservatively as 10% effective
