# UKES Outreach Emails — Pre-Application Sequence

A sequence of three emails for engaging UK Endorsement Services Ltd (UKES) ahead of formally submitting the Innovator Founder endorsement application. The sequence is designed to:

1. Open a respectful, brief first contact;
2. Request a 30-minute pre-application call (which UKES typically welcomes);
3. Confirm the call and pre-share the executive summary.

The applicant should send these from a professional UK domain (e.g. `quadri.yusuf@dequad.com`) and reference the live product at `https://dequad.co.uk`.

## Tone-and-style notes for the applicant

- **Be brief.** UKES assessors review hundreds of pitches. Aim for emails that fit on one screen.
- **Lead with the live product link.** A working product is the strongest credibility signal an Innovator Founder applicant can bring.
- **Use UK English** (organisation, not organization; pilot programme, not program).
- **Avoid hype words.** UKES is allergic to "revolutionary", "disruptive", "Uber-for-X". Stick to verifiable claims.
- **Don't request a decision in writing without paying the assessment fee.** UKES's published process requires a paid submission to obtain a written assessment.
