## 01 · The problem

> **54%** of UK undergraduates report frequent loneliness *(ONS, 2023)*
> **34%** experience a clinically-significant mental-health issue during their degree *(Student Minds, 2024)*
> **6.1 weeks** average wait for a university counsellor *(UUK, 2024)*

University wellbeing teams are reactive. By the time a student presents in crisis, the relationships that would have prevented it never formed.

**No-one is solving this for the 95% of students who never walk into the counselling office.**

## 02 · Why now

- **Office for Students (2023)** — every UK uni must now evidence proactive prevention.
- **Online Safety Act 2023** — student-facing platforms must implement risk-assessment and reporting flows. DEQUAD ships with these built in.
- **UUK Mental Health Charter** — **100+ UK universities** committed to data-driven wellbeing strategy.
- **Post-pandemic loneliness has not normalised** — undergraduate levels remain **18 percentage points** above 2019 baseline.

The buyer is now mandated to spend, and the regulator is now watching. The window to land institutional contracts is open today.

## 03 · The product

DEQUAD is **one app, four shipped modules**, live in production at dequad.co.uk.

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Verified peer matching** | `.ac.uk` email + student-attestation; match by intent (friend / study / support). |
| **Daily wellbeing tracker** | 30-second mood, sleep, stress and connection check-in → personal trend dashboard. |
| **Machine-assisted safeguarding** | LLM classifier on mood + chat metadata flags risk signals; webhook to university DSL in **< 60s**. |
| **University Insights Dashboard** | Anonymised, aggregated wellbeing analytics — cohort mood trends, hotspot alerts. |

Built with FastAPI + MongoDB + Expo (universal iOS / Android / web).

## 11 · The plan · self-funded, no ask

**There is no funding ask.** DEQUAD's 3-year plan is funded entirely by **£6,000 of founder capital plus revenue** — no pre-seed, seed or other external investment is assumed, committed, or required for it to succeed.

### Why this is the plan

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Costs are scoped to fit £6,000** | Y1 total spend ≈£4,700 — hosting, tooling, basic legal/insurance, minimal founder-led marketing. No paid hires, no office lease. |
| **No dependency on the Bedfordshire pilot** | Nothing is signed. If it converts, it's Y2+ upside (Section 11). If it doesn't, the plan is unaffected. |
| **Growth is sequenced to revenue, not capital** | The plan's only hire (Y3, part-time) and modest founder pay are triggered by revenue actually landing — not by a funding round closing. |

If institutional traction significantly exceeds this conservative forecast (5+ paying universities), the founders may explore external investment beyond Year 3 to accelerate growth — a future option, not a plan dependency.
