# 1. Executive Summary

**Dequad** is a UK-built, mobile-first student wellbeing platform that combines three core pillars — **mood tracking, Hinge-style peer matching, and institutional safeguarding alerts** — into a single secure mobile app exclusively for UK university students.

The UK is in a **measurable student mental health crisis**: 57% of UK students report a mental health issue (Student Minds, 2023), 50% experience loneliness (ONS), and university counselling waiting times average **4–6 weeks**. Existing solutions — generic meditation apps, dating apps, and overburdened campus counselling — are reactive, not proactive, and not student-verified.

**Dequad addresses this gap** with:
- A `.ac.uk` verified student network (safety + trust by design)
- Daily mood tracking with early-warning analytics for university welfare teams
- End-to-end encrypted peer connection (Hinge-style prompts and comments, not photo-swipe)
- Automated safeguarding keyword detection aligned to **OfS B3 conditions** and the **UUK Mental Health Charter**

**Innovation**: Dequad is the **first UK platform** to combine proactive peer connection, mood data, and institutional safeguarding into one student-verified mobile experience. No direct UK competitor offers this combination.

**Viability**: The founder brings [insert founder's relevant degree, work experience, technical skill set, and prior venture/product experience here]. A working MVP (React Native + FastAPI + MongoDB) is already built and deployable.

**Scalability**: Dequad targets the UK higher education sector exclusively — **2.86 million students across 285+ institutions** (HESA 2023/24). The 5-year plan creates **20+ UK-based skilled jobs**, generates **£3.75M ARR by Year 5**, and serves **750,000+ UK students**.

**The ask**: £500,000 seed investment (a portion of which is the founder's own and qualifying co-investment funds as per Innovator Founder rules). Use of funds: product (40%), UK growth (30%), UK team (20%), legal & compliance (10%).

# 4. The Solution & Innovation — Innovation Criterion

> *The Innovator Founder route requires a genuine, original, novel business idea that meets new or existing market needs and creates a competitive advantage.*

## 4.1 Product Overview

Dequad is a **mobile-first** (iOS + Android), `.ac.uk` email-verified application with three integrated pillars:

### Pillar 1 — Mood Tracking & Insights
- Daily 30-second mood check-ins with contextual prompts
- Pattern recognition over time (sleep, social, academic context)
- UK-relevant signposting (NHS, Samaritans 116 123, Shout 85258, Student Minds)
- **Anonymised aggregate analytics** delivered to university welfare teams (institutional value)

### Pillar 2 — Meaningful Peer Connections
- **Hinge-style** profile browsing (not Tinder-style photo-swipe)
- Like a specific **prompt response or interest**, with a comment — encourages substantive interaction
- Match by university, course, year of study, interests
- **End-to-end encrypted (AES-256)** messaging
- All users **verified `.ac.uk` UK university email** — closed, trusted network

### Pillar 3 — Safeguarding & Institutional Integration
- **Keyword detection** for crisis indicators (suicide, self-harm, severe distress) aligned to UK welfare practice
- **Automated alerts** to designated university safeguarding leads via SMTP
- **In-app crisis resources** surfaced instantly to the user
- **Audit trail** maintained for OfS B3 condition compliance
- **University dashboard**: anonymised analytics + alert management for welfare teams

## 4.2 Why This Is Innovative — Per Home Office Criterion

The endorsement criterion requires "**a genuine, original, novel business idea**" that creates "**a competitive advantage**." Dequad meets this on multiple, verifiable dimensions:

### 4.2.1 Novel Combination
No UK-available product combines **all three** of: verified-student peer connection, longitudinal mood data, and automated institutional safeguarding. Each element exists separately; Dequad is the first to integrate them.

### 4.2.2 Novel Verification Model
Dequad's `.ac.uk` verification creates a **closed, trusted UK-student-only network**. This is structurally different from generic dating apps (Tinder/Bumble) and from anonymous mental health forums (TogetherAll).

### 4.2.3 Novel Data Flow for Safeguarding
The **proactive keyword detection → automated SMTP alert → welfare team dashboard** flow does not currently exist in UK university tech stacks. Most universities still rely on **reactive** self-reporting via web forms or in-person appointments.

### 4.2.4 Novel UX for Student Mental Health
"Like a prompt, not a photo" is a meaningful UX shift away from appearance-based matching, which evidence shows is correlated with poorer mental health outcomes in young users (Royal Society for Public Health, *#StatusOfMind*, 2017).

### 4.2.5 Defensible Competitive Advantage
- **Data network effect**: more universities → more anonymised benchmarking value → more universities sign up
- **Trust moat**: `.ac.uk` verification is hard for international competitors to replicate without UK partnerships
- **Regulatory alignment**: built around UK-specific OfS / ICO / UUK frameworks — a structural advantage over US-built products entering the UK
