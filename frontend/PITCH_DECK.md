# Dequad Pitch Deck
## The Wellbeing & Connection Platform for UK University Students

---

## Slide 1: The Problem

### A UK Student Mental Health Crisis

- **57%** of UK students report mental health issues (Student Minds, 2023)
- **1 in 3** UK undergraduates experience serious psychological distress
- **50%** feel lonely most or all of the time at university (ONS, UK)
- **4–6 weeks** average waiting time for UK university counselling services
- **319%** rise in students disclosing a mental health condition over the past decade (HESA)

> "The transition into UK university life is one of the most mentally challenging periods in a young person's life — and the current support system can't keep up."

---

## Slide 2: Our Solution

### Dequad — Proactive Wellbeing for UK Students

A UK-built, UK-focused mobile platform that combines:

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Daily Mood Tracking | Early detection of mental health trends |
| Student Matching (Hinge-style) | Combat loneliness through meaningful peer connections |
| Encrypted Chat | Safe, private communication |
| Safeguarding Alerts | Automatic escalation to UK university welfare teams |
| University Integration | Verified `.ac.uk` student community |

---

## Slide 3: Key Features

### Hinge-Style Student Matching
- Connect based on course, interests and study style
- Like specific profile prompts with comments — not just photos
- Verified UK university students only (`.ac.uk` email)

### Intelligent Mood Tracking
- Daily check-ins with contextual prompts
- Pattern recognition over time
- Wellness resources tailored to mood trends

### Safeguarding System
- Keyword detection aligned to UK welfare frameworks
- Automatic alerts to university designated safeguarding leads
- 24/7 in-app crisis resources (Samaritans, Shout, NHS 111)
