# Educare Pitch Deck
## Your Wellbeing Companion for University Life

---

## 🎯 Slide 1: The Problem

### Student Mental Health Crisis

- **75%** of mental health conditions develop before age 24
- **1 in 3** university students experience significant mental health issues
- **50%** of students feel lonely or isolated at university
- **Long waiting times** for campus counseling services (4-6 weeks average)
- **Stigma** prevents students from seeking help

> "The transition to university is one of the most mentally challenging periods in a young person's life."

---

## 💡 Slide 2: Our Solution

### Educare - Proactive Student Wellbeing

A mobile-first platform that combines:

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|--------|
| 📊 **Daily Mood Tracking** | Early detection of mental health trends |
| 👥 **Student Matching** | Combat loneliness through meaningful connections |
| 💬 **Encrypted Chat** | Safe, private communication |
| 🚨 **Safeguarding Alerts** | Automatic escalation for at-risk students |
| 🎓 **University Integration** | Verified student community |

## ✨ Slide 3: Key Features

### Hinge-Style Student Matching
- Connect based on interests, course, and study style
- Like specific parts of profiles with comments
- University-verified students only

### Intelligent Mood Tracking
- Daily check-ins with personalized prompts
- Pattern recognition over time
- Wellness resources based on mood trends

### Safeguarding System
- Keyword detection for crisis situations
- Automatic alerts to university welfare teams
- Crisis resources always accessible
