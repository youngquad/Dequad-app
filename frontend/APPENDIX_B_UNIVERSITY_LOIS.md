## 3. What to Request from a Signing University

To make each LOI **maximally credible** to the Home Office endorsing body, the founder should ensure each letter includes:

| Element | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| **University official letterhead** | Authenticity |
| **Named signatory with title** | Accountability (not just a generic "welfare team") |
| **Specific student-cohort target number** | Demonstrates concrete pilot scope |
| **Pilot academic year specified** | Time-bound commitment |
| **Reference to the UK welfare context** | Shows the university recognises the UK problem space |
| **Non-binding language** | Realistic — endorsers know LOIs are not contracts |
| **Reference to DPIA / OfS compliance** | Shows the university takes governance seriously, lending credibility |
| **University contact email and phone** | Endorser may verify directly |

## 4. Outreach Process (Demonstrating Founder Market Awareness)

The founder's UK university outreach follows a structured process. Documenting this is itself evidence of **Viability**:

### Stage 1 — Identify Decision-Maker
Use the [University] "Student Services" / "Wellbeing" / "Mental Health" public pages to identify the named lead. Target seniority: **Head of / Director of**.

### Stage 2 — Warm Introduction
Where possible, request introduction via:
- AMOSSHE (UK Student Services professional body)
- UMHAN (University Mental Health Advisers Network)
- Student Minds (UK student MH charity)
- Personal network from founder's own UK university experience

### Stage 3 — First-Meeting Agenda (30-min Zoom)
1. UK student MH context (jointly understood)
2. Dequad's proposed value proposition (5 mins)
3. **Live demo** of MVP (10 mins)
4. Welfare team's current pain points (university speaks 10 mins)
5. Proposed pilot structure (5 mins)

### Stage 4 — Follow-Up Pack
Sent within 24 hours of meeting:
- One-page summary of Dequad value proposition
- Draft pilot terms
- Draft DPIA outline
- Link to Dequad website
- LOI template (this document)

### Stage 5 — LOI Signing
2–6 week typical timeline. Universities often need internal sign-off from Legal / IT / DPO before signing even a non-binding LOI.

### Stage 6 — Move to Pilot Contract
Once LOI signed, transition to:
- Full DPIA
- Standard university procurement workflow
- Information Security Questionnaire (ISQ)
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Pilot launch event
