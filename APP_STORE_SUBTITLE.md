# DEQUAD — App Store Subtitle

Paste into App Store Connect → Your App → App Information → **Subtitle** (30-character limit, appears directly under the app name on the product page).

## Recommended

```
Wellbeing & connection for uni
```

30/30 characters. Leads with wellbeing (mood check-ins, safeguarding) rather than "dating," matching how `APP_STORE_PRIVACY.md` says the listing should be positioned given Apple's elevated review for mental-health claims and user-generated content — while still naming DEQUAD's other core feature, matching with other students.

## Alternatives

| Subtitle | Chars |
|---|---|
| `Connect & thrive at university` | 30 |
| `Wellbeing for university life` | 29 |
| `Your uni wellbeing companion` | 28 |
| `Match, chat & thrive at uni` | 27 |

All fit within Apple's 30-character cap.
