# DEQUAD — Founder Story Reel (90 seconds)

**Title**: *"Why we built DEQUAD — from two final-year students who almost dropped out."*
**Format**: 9:16 vertical · TikTok-native · Instagram Reels · YouTube Shorts
**Cast**: Yusuf Quadri (Founder/CEO) + Yusuff Adeagbo (CTO)
**Tone**: Honest, raw, no startup-bro polish. Imperfect lighting is FINE. Phone selfie + tripod for some shots.
**Music**: Soft acoustic guitar build (e.g. Hozier-instrumental style). Drops to ambient strings at the emotional beat. Lifts to upbeat indie-pop at the CTA.

## 🎙️ Direction notes for the founders

1. **Don't memorize. Use the script as a guide.** Authenticity > polish. Trip over your words occasionally.
2. **Eye contact with the lens.** Imagine you're talking to one specific student, not a crowd.
3. **The SU President line is the emotional core.** Slow down there. Don't smile through it.
4. **Wardrobe**: hoodie + jeans, NOT a suit. You're founders, not bankers.
5. **Location swap**: the founders' Bedfordshire campus shots can be substituted with the NatWest accelerator London office if filming there is easier.
6. **Don't say "we're disrupting"** — ever. The word "disrupt" instantly TikTok-flags content as inauthentic startup-bro nonsense.
