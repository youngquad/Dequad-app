## 💬 First-comment hack (TikTok)

TikTok algo rewards engagement on the **first comment**. After posting, immediately comment from your own admin account something like:

> Drop a 🥀 if you needed this today
> Tag the friend you'd match with first 👇
> Anyone else at @[a specific uni] feeling this? 🇬🇧

This artificially boosts the comment-velocity in the first 10 minutes — usually 2–3× the reach.
